Lab 04 · Multiple underlyings

Baskets & Worst-Of

Three names do not automatically create diversification. Compare a true equal-weight basket, where gains and losses offset, with a worst-of reference set, where one weak name controls every test.

Scope:Three equally normalised underlyings, common ATM volatility and equicorrelation, zero-drift flat-vol or downside local-vol paths, conditional coupons without memory, one initial non-call observation and a European downside barrier. The skew comes from the Volatility Skew lab. Names are fictional labels; custom weights, quanto effects, dividends, issuer default and fees are omitted.

What does correlation change?

Low correlation creates more dispersion between names. That can help an average basket while making it easier for a worst-of structure to find one weak constituent.

Equal-weight basket

The reference is the arithmetic average of the three normalised levels. Appreciation in one name can offset depreciation in another.

Worst-of reference

The lowest-performing name controls the coupon, call and maturity tests. Gains in the other two names do not compensate for it.

Correlation

Correlation describes co-movement, not identical outcomes. Lower correlation usually creates a wider gap between the strongest and weakest constituent.
