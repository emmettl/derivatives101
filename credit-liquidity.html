Promise, Price & Recovery

A payoff formula says what the issuer promises. It does not guarantee the issuer can pay, that somebody will quote today, or that an early exit will occur at model value.

Specification discipline

Keep the concepts separate

Each number answers a different question and needs different evidence.

ConceptQuestion answeredWhat it is not

Protection is conditional on payment

“100% capital protected” describes the contractual redemption if the issuer performs. The note is still an unsecured obligation.

Model value is not a quote

A valuation can exist when no dealer is willing or obliged to transact. A quote may also include an exit cost.

Recovery is a separate process

After default, legal claim, ranking, resolution timing and realised recovery replace the original payoff timetable.

Credit and liquidity references

The SEC’s structured-notes investor bulletin explains that principal protection is subject to issuer credit risk, structured notes are unsecured obligations, and secondary-market trading may be very limited. FINRA’s principal-protection guidance similarly distinguishes the issuer’s promise from its financial strength and notes that an issuer may be willing—but is not obliged—to provide an indicative bid. Actual legal ranking, claim amount, recovery and timing depend on the specific issuer and documentation.
