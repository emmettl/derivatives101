Model and accounting rules

What this experiment assumes

These assumptions are part of the result. Change the implementation rules and the reported P&L changes too.

RuleUsed hereWhat a real specification must resolve

Gamma makes delta move

The call’s delta changes with spot and time. A hedge set yesterday is generally not neutral today.

Realised versus implied

The premium is set using implied volatility; hedge trading experiences the realised path. Their difference matters, but does not determine every discrete-path result on its own.

More hedging means more trading

Frequent rebalancing follows model delta more closely, while increasing turnover and transaction-cost exposure.

Hedging references

The Options Industry Council’s Greeks and risk-management overview explains that delta can indicate the shares needed for a hedge and that gamma makes the required hedge change. Its volatility and Greeks guide also notes that Black–Scholes is useful but does not perfectly describe real markets. The Options Clearing Corporation’s current options disclosure document is the authoritative risk disclosure for US exchange-traded options. This lab is a simplified European simulation and omits gaps, bid/offer spreads beyond the selected cost, dividends, volatility surfaces, funding constraints, market impact and execution risk.
