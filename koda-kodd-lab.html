When does the quantity jump?

The knock-out ends future trades. The forward price decides whether an active fixing uses the base or geared quantity. A guarantee can keep base-size trades alive briefly after a knock-out.

Gearing changes quantity

The forward price does not move. On the adverse side, the same price applies to a larger number of units, so exposure grows precisely when the market has moved against the investor.

Knock-out stops the future

A knock-out normally prevents the current and later trades; it does not reverse units already bought or sold. Guaranteed opening observations can modify that rule.

KODD is the mirror—with no ceiling

A decumulator sells more units when spot rises above its forward price. The opportunity cost of selling below market has no fixed upper bound if the underlying keeps rising.

Mechanics references

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority’s current accumulator guidance covers periodic purchases, knock-out cessation and multiplier conditions. The SFC–HKMA thematic review explains that the discount reflects option premium and that accumulators and decumulators can amplify losses.
