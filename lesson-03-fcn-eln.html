The same economics, several market names

An FCN is typically a fixed-coupon, worst-of note with a knock-in barrier, autocall and physical settlement. A classic ELN is simpler: buy at a discount and receive shares if the stock closes below the strike. ELI and ELS can describe regulatory wrappers or regional forms rather than fundamentally new payoffs.

Nobody polices the marketing names. Read the term sheet, not the label.
FCN

Fixed coupon note: usually a short-dated worst-of basket with unconditional coupons.

ELN

Equity linked note: usually a single-name, one-observation discount structure.

ELI

Equity linked investment: a regulated retail wrapper that may add airbag and autocall terms.

ELS

Equity-linked securities: in Korea, often longer-dated index autocalls sold to retail clients.

The coupon is not the principal

One weak name can decide the whole note

The word “fixed” truthfully describes the coupon and says nothing about principal. The basket’s weakest performer usually controls the coupon test, autocall test and maturity loss. Stronger constituents become irrelevant once the worst name is identified.

What makes the wrapper operationally different

Physical settlement is not a footnote

If the note loses, the client may receive the worst-performing shares at the original strike. Share count is typically notional divided by strike price, rounded to the market’s board lot, with residual cash handled separately. Holidays, settlement cycles and corporate actions can all affect delivery.

Share count

Notional ÷ (strike percentage × initial price), then rounded under the contract.

Delivered value

The fixed share count valued at the weaker maturity price.

A note can pay every coupon and still generate a large overall loss. Coupons and principal are separate ledgers; add them only at the end.

Takeaways

What to remember

Fixed describes coupon

It does not mean fixed principal or fixed total return.

Worst-of discards good news

One weak name can outweigh every strong constituent.

Settlement creates real work

Share rounding and market conventions belong in the payoff specification.

Names are regional

Classify the economic terms before relying on the wrapper label.
