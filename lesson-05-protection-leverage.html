Capital-protected notes

Buy the floor first, then spend what is left

To promise 100 at maturity, the issuer sets aside the present value of that amount on its own funding curve. The residual issue proceeds, after fees, buy call options. Participation is not an independent feature: it is the option budget divided by the price of one unit of upside.

Participation is a residual, not a feature.

Higher rates make the future repayment cheaper today, leaving more option budget. Lower protection, longer tenor or an upside cap can also improve participation, but each changes the risk or opportunity set.

Four caveats

What “capital protected” does not mean

Maturity only

The note can trade below par before maturity as rates and option value change.

Issuer credit

The promise is an unsecured claim, not collateral or deposit insurance.

Bond benchmark

A flat underlying can mean zero note return while the issuer’s plain bond earns yield.

Fee drag

Fees reduce the option budget and participation, not the stated floor.

Mini futures

A small certificate controls a large exposure

A long mini future is worth roughly the underlying minus its financing level. If spot is 100 and financing is 90, the certificate costs about 10 and provides roughly ten-times leverage. A stop-loss above the financing level terminates the product before the gap becomes negative.

Financing level

The issuer-funded portion. It accretes by the reference rate plus spread.

Stop-loss

The trigger that ends the product; market gaps can reduce or erase residual value.

The carry charge is embedded by moving the financing level, so the certificate can lose value even if the underlying is unchanged. A mini future may retain a residual after its stop-loss; a knock-out warrant commonly expires worthless at its barrier.

Takeaways

What to remember

Floor first

Funding determines what remains available for upside.

Protection is a promise

Issuer default can defeat the maturity floor.

Leverage is a ratio

Small price divided into full exposure produces a large multiplier.

Carry moves the strike

Mini-future financing accrues even when the market stands still.
