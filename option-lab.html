What this teaches—and what it does not

A model is a lens.

Black–Scholes is powerful because it turns a handful of assumptions into a complete value surface and a hedge. Real markets add changing volatility, jumps, liquidity, discrete hedging and transaction costs. Use this lab to build intuition, not to infer a tradeable price.
