Lab 17 · Multi-leg option strategies

Options à la carte

A strategy is just a list of contracts and signs. Combine up to four calls or puts, add optional knock-in or knock-out barriers, then see how each leg shapes the result before and at expiry.

Scope:European cash-settled options on one normalized underlying. Premiums use Black–Scholes for vanillas and a deliberately simplified barrier weighting. The expiry result is exact for the selected path extremes; the premium is illustrative, not tradeable.

Add the legs

The strategy payoff is the signed sum of every contract payoff. Long quantities are positive; short quantities are negative.

Premium moves break-even

A payoff diagram starts at zero. A profit-and-loss diagram also includes what was paid or received to open the position.

Barriers remember the path

Knock-ins need a qualifying touch to activate. Knock-outs cease to participate after one, even if the terminal spot later recovers.

Strategy references

The Options Industry Council strategy catalogue describes common recipes including long calls, straddles, strangles and butterflies, while its profit-and-loss graph guide illustrates how spreads combine into characteristic shapes. FINRA’s options overview notes that multi-leg strategies can bound some gains and losses but introduce their own risks, and its assignment guidance explains why one short leg can create obligations independently of the overall strategy. This lab avoids that mismatch by using European, cash-settled options with one expiry.
