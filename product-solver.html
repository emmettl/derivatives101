The snapshot lives in one file, data/market-snapshot.json. Replace any value with an observed one and the whole lab reprices. Spot levels are cosmetic: every product is quoted as a percentage of the initial level, so rates, dividend yields, volatilities and skew are what move the answers.

Educational model only. Illustrative snapshot, simplified pricing and no hedging, tax or liquidity costs. Not a price, a quote or investment advice.
