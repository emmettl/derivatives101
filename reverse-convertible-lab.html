No barrier

The investor is exposed below the strike at maturity. There is no knock-in condition and therefore no safety buffer.

Issuer callable

An eligible date gives the issuer a right. Statistics require an explicit issuer-exercise assumption because the market path alone does not determine the decision.

Autocallable

The terms define a market test. If it passes, redemption is automatic and future coupons disappear.
