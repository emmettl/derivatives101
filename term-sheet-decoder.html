    Definition of ready

    What belongs in the specification?

    Payoff language is only one part. A buildable requirement also identifies data, dates, state, exceptional events and settlement outputs.

    Explicitly outside this fictional case

    Corporate-action adjustments, tax treatment, issuer default, hedging, secondary-market valuation and jurisdiction-specific legal drafting.

    Names are shorthand

    “Autocallable reverse convertible” identifies a family. It does not settle comparison operators, calendars, ordering or delivery mechanics.

    Boundaries reveal ambiguity

    Test the exact barrier, a same-day coupon and call, a missing fixing and a fractional delivery entitlement. Ordinary examples often hide the gaps.

    State must persist

    A barrier breach and a memory balance survive beyond the observation that created them. Recording only current inputs cannot reproduce the contract.

    Reference examples

    This is a fictional case rather than a model term sheet. Public filings show why the details matter: this SEC-filed autocallable note specifies inclusive comparisons, coupon treatment on a call date and postponement for disruption events. FINRA’s structured-note overview explains that final documents also need clear treatment of issuer credit, initial value, fees and liquidity. Exact conventions are product-specific.
