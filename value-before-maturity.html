Value Before Maturity

Capital protection describes a payment at maturity, subject to issuer performance. It does not promise that the note can be sold for 100 today.

Scope:A fictional five-year, 100% capital-protected participation note. The model separates a credit-adjusted bond and call option, then deducts an illustrative exit cost. It is not a tradable valuation.

Protection has a date

The 100 floor applies at contractual maturity and remains an unsecured promise of the issuer. It is not a continuous market-value floor.

Spot is only one input

Time, rates, dividends, volatility and issuer spread can offset or magnify the effect of the underlying—even when spot does not move.

A bid is another layer

A model value is not necessarily executable. Liquidity, size, dealer spread and unwind costs can create another deduction.

Valuation references

FINRA’s structured-note overview explains that initial estimated value is generally below issue price and that an early sale may occur at a substantial discount. A recent SEC-filed pricing supplement describes how funding rates, credit, hedging costs, fees and dealer discounts can affect estimated and secondary-market values. Exact issuer models and bid conventions differ.
