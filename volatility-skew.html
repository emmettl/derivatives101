Specification discipline

“Use market volatility” is not executable

A valuation specification must identify the curve, mapping and fallback rules—not just name a volatility source.

Decision Question to resolve Why it matters

A curve is only one expiry

This page shows volatility across strikes at one tenor. Add many expiries and the collection becomes a volatility surface.

Skew is not a direction forecast

Higher downside-put volatility means those options are richer in implied-volatility terms. It does not by itself predict where the underlying will finish.

Terms inherit model choices

Coupon and participation are outputs of option budgets. Strike-specific volatility, interpolation and market-side conventions can change them.

Volatility-skew references

The Options Industry Council’s skew overview defines skew as the difference between implied volatilities at different strikes for the same expiry and distinguishes flat, smile and smirk shapes. Its volatility and skew teaching summary explains that market-implied volatility varies across strikes rather than remaining flat. For an example of production detail, Cboe’s implied-volatility methodology explicitly specifies interpolation and extrapolation techniques. This lab uses neither market data nor that methodology.
